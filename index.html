Retail Market Customer Segmentation Dashboard (Unsupervised Learning)

Artifact Overview & Implementation Specs

1. Customization for the Audience

The target audience for this artifact was retail marketing strategists looking to design targeted promotional campaigns. I tailored the cluster visualization outputs into clean pivot tables and column charts, clearly labeling demographic brackets (age, gender, spending score) so marketers could immediately identify distinct customer personas without needing a deep background in clustering algorithms.

2. Lessons Learned

This project deepened my understanding of unsupervised learning, specifically the nuances of K-Means clustering and distance-based feature scaling. I learned how to use the Elbow Method to evaluate mathematical inertia and determine the optimal number of clusters ($k$). I realized that unsupervised learning requires combining statistical validation with domain knowledge to ensure the discovered clusters are practically meaningful.

3. Feedback and Revisions

Feedback on the preliminary draft noted that the raw clusters were hard to interpret without visual context. I incorporated this by adding interactive bar charts and comparative spending breakdown tables. I also refined the cluster labeling to directly describe consumer behavior (e.g., "High-Income, High-Spending"), making the insights instantly actionable for marketing applications.

Automated AI Resume & Candidate Screening Tool (NLP & Semantic Search)

Artifact Overview & Implementation Specs

1. Customization for the Audience

I designed this tool specifically for technical recruiters and hiring managers who need to evaluate large volumes of job applications efficiently. Rather than presenting abstract vector distances, I customized the output to display percentage match scores and highlighted keyword alignments, allowing recruiters to quickly understand why a candidate was ranked at a specific level.

2. Lessons Learned

This artifact significantly expanded my practical knowledge of Natural Language Processing (NLP) and transformer-based architectures. I learned how to preprocess unstructured text, build tokenization pipelines, and generate dense sentence embeddings using Hugging Face models. Calculating cosine similarity across vector embeddings gave me hands-on experience in modern semantic search workflows.

3. Feedback and Revisions

Initial testing showed that simple keyword matching struggled with varied resume terminology (e.g., "ML Engineer" vs. "Machine Learning Practitioner"). Following feedback, I shifted from traditional keyword TF-IDF parsing to pre-trained transformer embeddings (Sentence-BERT). This revision substantially improved the tool's ability to capture semantic intent regardless of phrasing differences.

Customer Churn Prediction System (Classification)

Artifact Overview & Implementation Specs

1. Customization for the Audience

To ensure this artifact resonated with business retention teams and non-technical stakeholders, I tailored the outputs to focus heavily on actionable metrics—specifically Precision, Recall, and ROC-AUC—rather than raw loss values. Instead of presenting purely complex mathematical formulas, I provided an intuitive decision threshold slider within the interactive interface, allowing retention managers to adjust risk tolerance based on budget and customer acquisition costs.

2. Lessons Learned

Building this classification pipeline taught me the critical importance of handling class imbalance in real-world tabular data. I learned how naive accuracy can be misleading when predicting rare events like customer churn. Working through feature scaling and hyperparameter tuning deepened my understanding of model evaluation trade-offs and taught me how to properly structure cross-validation to prevent data leakage.

3. Feedback and Revisions

Initial feedback highlighted that early iterations of the feature set relied too heavily on correlated variables, which reduced model interpretability. In response, I conducted collinearity checks and removed redundant features. I also incorporated feedback on the user interface by adding visual feature importance plots, making it easier for stakeholders to see which behavioral factors (e.g., contract length or support ticket frequency) drove the risk scores.

Predictive Maintenance & Demand Forecasting Model (Time-Series)

Artifact Overview & Implementation Specs

1. Customization for the Audience

To meet the needs of supply chain managers and logistics operators, I focused the model outputs on time-horizon demand forecasts and risk alert thresholds. I presented the results using intuitive line graphs comparing historical demand against forecasted trends, alongside clear error metrics (MAE and RMSE), enabling operators to make informed inventory decisions.

2. Lessons Learned

This project provided valuable experience in handling time-dependent, sequential data structures. I learned how to engineer temporal lag features, compute rolling averages, and account for seasonality without introducing future data leakage into training splits. It taught me the practical complexities of maintaining model stability over dynamic time horizons.

3. Feedback and Revisions

Early feedback indicated that the baseline model was overshooting predictions during sudden demand spikes. I revised the approach by testing different lag windows and introducing rolling statistics to smooth out short-term variance. Incorporating these revisions reduced prediction error significantly and made the forecasts far more reliable for operational planning.
